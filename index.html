01 / About

A little about me

I am a PhD researcher in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at IIT Bombay, working with Prof. Vishnu Narayanan and Prof. Avinash Bhardwaj.

My research asks when the hidden structure of a discrete problem makes a continuous relaxation exact. I am particularly interested in Max-Cut and Max-k-Cut, optimality certificates, and the geometry of semidefinite programming.

I also study game-theoretic aspects of graph partitioning, including the relationship between optimal solutions and coalitional stability.

Outside research, I watch a lot of movies and made two zero-budget short animated films during my undergraduate years: Tinkle—the Superhero and A River Island Tale. The latter won third prize at the Brahmaputra Valley Film Festival in 2015, which was attended by Rajkumar Hirani and Shimit Amin.
